Terms of service

Effective November 11, 2026

These terms describe use of the Upgrade Lab Gaming channel publishing tool, maintained by the channel operator, and its informational website.

Purpose and authorized use

The tool renders original vehicle game simulations and uploads owner-approved videos to the channel authorized through Google OAuth. The channel owner controls the connected account, approved batches, audience declarations, visibility settings, and publication schedule.

Content and account responsibilities

The operator is responsible for reviewing video content and metadata, holding the necessary rights to uploaded material, and selecting accurate audience and disclosure settings. Use of YouTube through this tool is subject to the YouTube Terms of Service and applicable YouTube policies.

Scheduling and availability

Publishing depends on the VPS, network connectivity, Google authorization, YouTube upload limits, processing, and platform availability. The tool records confirmed upload responses and can pause remaining work after an upload-limit response. The channel owner can inspect final video status through YouTube Studio.

Data and service providers

The privacy policy describes the Google permissions, local records, and notifications used by this workflow. Google, YouTube, GitHub, the VPS host, and Telegram operate under their own applicable terms and policies.

Stopping the workflow

The operator can stop the server's publishing service and revoke the application's Google access. Video publication schedules and previously uploaded content can be managed through YouTube Studio.

Support and changes

Support requests can be raised through the project's GitHub issues. The operator may update these terms as the workflow changes. The effective date identifies the current published version.
